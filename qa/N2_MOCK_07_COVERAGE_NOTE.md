## Coverage decisions

The prior written bank repeats `っこない` four times; `にほかならない` and `ほかならない` appear three times each; several other patterns appear twice. M07 uses `っこない` once, avoids the repeated `にほかならない` family, and uses `につけ` once. That keeps legitimate syllabus overlap without leaning on the most repeated prior targets.

Reading passages are grouped into 11 distinct shared texts per mock. M07's unique passage lengths span 196–759 characters, median 451; prior medians span 463–563 characters. Length alone cannot establish JLPT difficulty, but M07 is not markedly shorter than the existing set. Its question types cover short, medium, integrated, long and information-retrieval reading.

The 30 scripts cover all five Mondai, including work coordination, commuting, housing, resident services and community facilities. Existing bank metadata does not tag Listening situation types consistently, so this pass cannot support a numerical claim about prior situation repetition. The full script text was compared for exact and near repeats.

The first blind solve flagged two M7 grammar questions as genuinely ambiguous and one collocation as awkward. All three were rewritten, then independently re-solved from a sealed three-item revision input. The star set was checked through sealed reviews; revisions 7–10 preserved answer-changing alternatives for M8_04 or M8_05 and were retained as negative evidence. The final version was reviewed blind with all 24 fragment orders assessed for both revised items; the reviewer found no natural or marginal alternative that changes either ★ answer. M5_L01 and M5_L02 require inference from the stated venue constraints rather than an explicit final-choice sentence; those remain evidence-based inference items. The current set has no human/native-speaker certification or calibrated difficulty ratings, so the review does not establish psychometric equivalence to an official JLPT form.

The mock uses the two N2 test sections and durations shown in [official JLPT guidance](https://www.jlpt.jp/e/guideline/testsections.html). The 75/30 item counts are this project’s practice blueprint; the official page specifies section structure and time, not an exact required question count for this mock.
